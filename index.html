Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working together.

How do customers open the 3D menu? +
We generate a custom QR code for your cafe tables or counter. Customers point their phone camera, and the menu immediately opens in their browser without downloading any apps.
How do WhatsApp orders arrive? +
When the customer picks their items and taps "Order on WhatsApp", an auto-formatted message with item names, quantities, and the total bill lands directly on your registered WhatsApp number.
How much time does it take to go live? +
Most single-page websites and digital menus are coded, tested, and deployed within 48 to 72 hours once menu details or photos are provided.
